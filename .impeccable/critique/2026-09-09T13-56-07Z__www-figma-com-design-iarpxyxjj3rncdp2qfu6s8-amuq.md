# AMUQ Stage 3 bounded critique and remediation

Method: two independent assessments followed by one bounded fix-and-reverification pass.

## Initial assessment

- Score: 23/40.
- Initial blocking findings: one broken critical prototype path and three P1 groups covering inconsistent generation steppers, inaccessible account menus, and clipped component variants.
- Confirmed strengths: Mammo-aligned learning layouts, concise copy, white application canvas, fixed global green accent, five non-green bank themes, and the retained black login brand panel.

## Fixes applied

- Reused the shared five-step numbered stepper on every generation prototype frame with the approved labels: 자료 추가, 챕터 분류, 문제 생성, 중복 검토, 최종 검토.
- Connected question generation, mini test, mock test, all three relay flows, final bank confirmation, and contextual profile menus.
- Added global and bank-context account menu overlay states with outside-click dismissal; the bank menu links to the learning record.
- Exposed all Field, Bank Card, and Study Mode variants by removing parent clipping and allowing the consolidated component canvas to hug its widest content.
